=========================================
KYC (KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER) GUIDE
=========================================

1. Introduction

Know Your Customer (KYC) is a mandatory verification process used by banks and financial institutions to verify the identity of customers before providing financial services.

KYC helps prevent:

• Identity theft
• Money laundering
• Financial fraud
• Terrorist financing
• Fake loan applications

-----------------------------------------

2. Objectives of KYC

The KYC process aims to:

• Verify customer identity
• Verify customer address
• Confirm date of birth
• Validate government-issued documents
• Prevent fraudulent applications
• Comply with regulatory requirements

-----------------------------------------

3. Required KYC Documents

Customers may be required to provide:

• PAN Card
• Aadhaar Card
• Passport
• Voter ID
• Driving Licence
• Passport-size Photograph
• Address Proof

-----------------------------------------

4. Information Verified

The bank verifies:

• Customer Name
• Date of Birth
• PAN Number
• Aadhaar Number
• Residential Address
• Photograph
• Signature (where applicable)

-----------------------------------------

5. OCR Verification

Uploaded documents are processed using Optical Character Recognition (OCR).

The OCR system extracts:

• Name
• PAN Number
• Aadhaar Number
• Date of Birth

The extracted information is compared with customer records.

-----------------------------------------

6. Matching Process

The KYC engine compares:

• Customer Name
• PAN Number
• Aadhaar Number
• Date of Birth

Each field is checked independently.

-----------------------------------------

7. Verification Status

Possible KYC outcomes include:

VERIFIED

All required fields match successfully.

-----------------------------------------

MANUAL REVIEW

One or more fields require human verification.

Common reasons include:

• OCR extraction errors
• Minor spelling differences
• Low OCR confidence
• Partial document visibility

-----------------------------------------

FAILED

Verification cannot be completed.

Possible reasons include:

• Invalid PAN
• Invalid Aadhaar
• Major document mismatch
• Fake document
• Missing mandatory information

-----------------------------------------

8. Similarity Score

The similarity score measures how closely the extracted document information matches the customer records.

Example:

100%

Perfect match.

95%

Very high match.

80%

Requires review.

Below acceptable threshold

Manual verification may be required.

-----------------------------------------

9. Name Matching

The system ignores minor formatting differences such as:

MOHAMED FAIZ

vs

Mohamed Faiz

However, significant spelling differences may require manual review.

-----------------------------------------

10. PAN Verification

PAN verification confirms:

• Correct PAN format
• Matching PAN number
• Consistency with customer records

Incorrect PAN details may delay loan approval.

-----------------------------------------

11. Aadhaar Verification

The Aadhaar number is checked for:

• Correct format
• Matching customer records
• OCR accuracy

Any mismatch may trigger manual verification.

-----------------------------------------

12. Date of Birth Verification

The Date of Birth extracted from uploaded documents is compared with the customer profile.

Any mismatch requires investigation before loan approval.

-----------------------------------------

13. Manual Review

Applications may be sent for manual review when:

• OCR confidence is low
• Document quality is poor
• Customer information partially matches
• Multiple documents contain inconsistent information

-----------------------------------------

14. Common Reasons for KYC Failure

• Incorrect PAN
• Incorrect Aadhaar
• Wrong Date of Birth
• Name mismatch
• Expired document
• Fake document
• Unreadable upload
• Missing mandatory document

-----------------------------------------

15. Customer Recommendations

Customers should:

• Upload clear images
• Use valid government documents
• Avoid cropped images
• Ensure all information is readable
• Verify details before submission

-----------------------------------------

16. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What does VERIFIED mean?

All mandatory customer details match successfully.

Q. What does MANUAL REVIEW mean?

The application requires verification by a bank officer before processing continues.

Q. Does MANUAL REVIEW mean my loan is rejected?

No. It simply means additional verification is required.

Q. Why is OCR confidence important?

Low OCR confidence may result in incorrect extraction and require manual verification.

Q. Can I upload a clearer document if verification fails?

Yes. Customers may be requested to upload a better-quality document.
