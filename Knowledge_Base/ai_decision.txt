=========================================
AI LOAN DECISION ENGINE GUIDE
=========================================

1. Introduction

The AI Loan Decision Engine assists the bank in evaluating loan applications by analyzing customer information, verification results, financial data, and risk indicators.

The AI provides a recommendation to support lending decisions. The final approval or rejection is made according to the bank's policies and review process.

-----------------------------------------

2. Purpose

The AI decision engine helps to:

• Improve consistency in loan evaluation
• Reduce manual effort
• Speed up application processing
• Support risk assessment
• Assist bank officers in decision-making

-----------------------------------------

3. Information Evaluated

The AI considers multiple inputs, including:

• Customer profile
• Loan application details
• Credit score
• Monthly income
• Existing financial obligations (if available)
• OCR results
• KYC verification
• Fraud assessment
• Internal lending policies

-----------------------------------------

4. Possible Recommendations

APPROVED

The application satisfies the bank's lending criteria.

MANUAL REVIEW

Additional verification is required before a final decision.

REJECTED

The application does not meet one or more lending requirements.

-----------------------------------------

5. Factors That Support Approval

Applications are more likely to be recommended for approval when they have:

• Strong credit score
• Stable income
• Successful KYC verification
• Accurate OCR extraction
• Low fraud risk
• Complete documentation

-----------------------------------------

6. Factors That May Require Manual Review

Examples include:

• Minor KYC mismatches
• Low OCR confidence
• Medium fraud risk
• Missing supporting documents
• Borderline credit score
• Inconsistent customer information

-----------------------------------------

7. Factors That May Lead to Rejection

Examples include:

• Very low credit score
• Failed KYC verification
• High fraud risk
• Forged or invalid documents
• Insufficient income
• Serious identity mismatches

-----------------------------------------

8. Relationship with Other Modules

The AI decision engine uses outputs from:

• OCR Module
• KYC Verification
• Fraud Detection
• Customer Database
• Loan Application Records

These components provide supporting information for the recommendation.

-----------------------------------------

9. Human Oversight

AI recommendations assist the lending process but do not replace human judgment.

Bank officers may:

• Approve the recommendation
• Request additional verification
• Override the recommendation when justified
• Reject an application based on policy

-----------------------------------------

10. Benefits

The AI decision engine provides:

• Faster processing
• Consistent evaluations
• Reduced manual workload
• Better risk identification
• Improved customer experience

-----------------------------------------

11. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Does the AI automatically approve loans?

No. The AI provides a recommendation that supports the bank's decision-making process.

Q. Can a recommendation change?

Yes. Additional documents, updated information, or manual review may change the final outcome.

Q. Why was my application marked for manual review?

Manual review is used when additional verification is needed before a decision can be made.

Q. Does a good credit score guarantee approval?

No. Credit score is important, but the bank also considers income, KYC, fraud analysis, documentation, and policy requirements.

Q. Can a bank officer override the AI recommendation?

Yes. Human reviewers can make the final decision based on policy and supporting evidence.
