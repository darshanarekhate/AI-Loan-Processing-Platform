=========================================
CAR LOAN POLICY
=========================================

1. Introduction

A Car Loan helps customers finance the purchase of a new or used vehicle. The bank offers competitive interest rates, flexible repayment options, and quick loan processing for eligible applicants.

-----------------------------------------

2. Eligible Applicants

• Salaried Employees
• Self-employed Professionals
• Business Owners
• Partnership Firms
• Companies (subject to policy)

-----------------------------------------

3. Minimum Age

21 Years

-----------------------------------------

4. Maximum Age at Loan Maturity

70 Years

-----------------------------------------

5. Minimum Monthly Income

₹25,000

Loan eligibility increases with higher and stable income.

-----------------------------------------

6. Eligible Vehicles

• New Cars
• Used Cars
• SUVs
• Hatchbacks
• Sedans
• Multi-Utility Vehicles (MUVs)

Commercial vehicles are generally covered under separate loan products.

-----------------------------------------

7. Loan Amount

The sanctioned amount depends on:

• Vehicle price
• Customer income
• Credit score
• Existing liabilities
• Repayment capacity

-----------------------------------------

8. Loan-to-Value (LTV)

The bank may finance up to 90% of the vehicle's on-road price, subject to policy and customer eligibility.

-----------------------------------------

9. Down Payment

Customers are generally required to contribute a minimum down payment based on the vehicle cost and financing terms.

-----------------------------------------

10. Loan Tenure

Minimum:
12 Months

Maximum:
84 Months

-----------------------------------------

11. Interest Rate

Interest rates depend on:

• Credit score
• Employment profile
• Loan tenure
• Vehicle category
• Bank policy

-----------------------------------------

12. Processing Fee

Typically ranges between:

0.25% and 1% of the sanctioned loan amount.

-----------------------------------------

13. Required Documents

• PAN Card
• Aadhaar Card
• Address Proof
• Passport-size Photograph
• Salary Slips or Income Proof
• Bank Statements
• Vehicle Quotation from Dealer

-----------------------------------------

14. Vehicle Insurance

Comprehensive insurance is generally mandatory before loan disbursement.

-----------------------------------------

15. Registration

The financed vehicle is registered according to applicable transport authority regulations.

The bank may record its interest on the vehicle until the loan is fully repaid.

-----------------------------------------

16. Repayment

Repayment is made through fixed monthly EMIs consisting of:

• Principal
• Interest

-----------------------------------------

17. Prepayment

Partial or full prepayment is permitted according to the loan agreement.

Applicable charges, if any, depend on prevailing bank policy.

-----------------------------------------

18. Foreclosure

Customers may close the loan before maturity after satisfying applicable foreclosure conditions.

-----------------------------------------

19. Common Reasons for Rejection

• Low credit score
• Insufficient income
• High debt obligations
• Incomplete documentation
• Failed KYC verification
• Fraud indicators
• Unstable employment history

-----------------------------------------

20. Benefits

• Quick approval process
• Flexible repayment tenure
• Competitive interest rates
• Financing for new and eligible used vehicles
• Convenient EMI repayment options

-----------------------------------------

21. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can I finance a used car?

Yes, eligible used vehicles may qualify subject to valuation and policy.

Q. Is a down payment required?

Yes, customers are generally expected to contribute a minimum down payment.

Q. Can I repay the loan early?

Yes, subject to prepayment or foreclosure terms.

Q. Is vehicle insurance mandatory?

Yes, comprehensive insurance is generally required.

Q. Does the bank finance the entire vehicle cost?

Financing is subject to the applicable Loan-to-Value (LTV) policy and customer eligibility.
