=========================================
OCR (OPTICAL CHARACTER RECOGNITION) GUIDE
=========================================

1. Introduction

Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is a technology that converts text from scanned documents and images into machine-readable data.

In the AI Loan Processing System, OCR is used to automatically extract customer information from uploaded identity documents.

-----------------------------------------

2. Purpose of OCR

OCR helps the bank to:

• Reduce manual data entry
• Speed up document verification
• Improve processing efficiency
• Support KYC verification
• Detect document inconsistencies

-----------------------------------------

3. Supported Documents

The OCR engine can process:

• PAN Card
• Aadhaar Card
• Passport
• Driving Licence
• Voter ID (where supported)

-----------------------------------------

4. Information Extracted

Depending on the document type, OCR may extract:

• Customer Name
• PAN Number
• Aadhaar Number
• Date of Birth
• Gender
• Address
• Document Number

-----------------------------------------

5. OCR Workflow

Step 1:
Customer uploads the document.

Step 2:
The image is preprocessed to improve readability.

Step 3:
OCR extracts the visible text.

Step 4:
The extracted data is structured into fields.

Step 5:
The extracted information is compared with customer records.

Step 6:
Results are sent to the KYC verification process.

-----------------------------------------

6. OCR Confidence Score

Every extracted document receives a confidence score.

Higher confidence indicates greater certainty that the extracted text is accurate.

Typical interpretation:

95% – 100%
Excellent extraction.

80% – 94%
Good extraction.

60% – 79%
Acceptable but may require review.

Below 60%
Manual verification is recommended.

-----------------------------------------

7. Factors Affecting OCR Accuracy

OCR accuracy depends on:

• Image quality
• Lighting
• Document orientation
• Image resolution
• Blur
• Shadows
• Cropped images
• Damaged documents

-----------------------------------------

8. Common OCR Errors

Possible extraction issues include:

• Incorrect character recognition
• Missing characters
• Incorrect PAN number
• Incorrect Aadhaar number
• Wrong Date of Birth
• Partial text extraction

-----------------------------------------

9. OCR Validation

The extracted information is compared with customer records.

Fields commonly validated include:

• Name
• PAN Number
• Aadhaar Number
• Date of Birth

Any mismatch may trigger manual review.

-----------------------------------------

10. OCR and KYC

OCR is the first step in the KYC verification process.

KYC uses the extracted information to determine whether customer records match the uploaded documents.

-----------------------------------------

11. Manual Review

Applications may be sent for manual verification if:

• OCR confidence is low
• Text is unreadable
• Multiple fields are missing
• Significant mismatches are detected

-----------------------------------------

12. Best Practices for Customers

For the best OCR results:

• Upload high-resolution images.
• Ensure the entire document is visible.
• Avoid glare and reflections.
• Keep the document flat.
• Use original documents.
• Do not crop important details.
• Capture images in good lighting.

-----------------------------------------

13. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What is OCR?

OCR converts text from images into editable and searchable digital text.

Q. Why is OCR used?

It reduces manual effort and speeds up document verification.

Q. What does the confidence score mean?

It indicates how accurately the OCR engine believes it extracted the document information.

Q. Can OCR make mistakes?

Yes. Poor image quality, blur, or damaged documents may reduce accuracy.

Q. What happens if OCR confidence is low?

The application may be sent for manual verification or the customer may be asked to upload a clearer document.
