=========================================
EDUCATION LOAN POLICY
=========================================

1. Introduction

An Education Loan helps students finance higher education in India or abroad. The loan may cover tuition fees, examination fees, hostel expenses, books, equipment, travel expenses for overseas education, and other approved educational costs.

-----------------------------------------

2. Eligible Applicants

• Indian Citizens
• Students admitted through merit or entrance examinations
• Students pursuing recognized courses
• Co-applicant (Parent/Guardian/Spouse) required

-----------------------------------------

3. Eligible Courses

• Undergraduate Degree
• Postgraduate Degree
• Diploma Courses
• Professional Courses
• Engineering
• Medical
• MBA
• Law
• Chartered Accountancy
• Government-approved vocational programs

-----------------------------------------

4. Domestic Education

Loans are available for approved institutions within India.

-----------------------------------------

5. Overseas Education

Loans are available for recognized universities abroad, subject to bank policy.

-----------------------------------------

6. Minimum Age

18 Years

Students below 18 years require a parent or legal guardian as the primary applicant.

-----------------------------------------

7. Maximum Loan Amount

The approved amount depends on:

• Course fees
• Living expenses
• Institution
• Repayment ability of co-applicant
• Credit assessment

-----------------------------------------

8. Expenses Covered

• Tuition Fees
• Hostel Fees
• Examination Fees
• Library Charges
• Laboratory Fees
• Books
• Laptop (where applicable)
• Uniform
• Travel Expenses
• Insurance Premium (if applicable)

-----------------------------------------

9. Margin Requirement

Depending on loan amount and bank policy, customers may need to contribute part of the educational expenses.

-----------------------------------------

10. Moratorium Period

Repayment generally starts after:

Course Completion
+
Specified Moratorium Period

Interest may continue during this period according to bank policy.

-----------------------------------------

11. Loan Tenure

Usually between:

5 Years

to

15 Years

depending on loan amount.

-----------------------------------------

12. Required Documents

• Admission Letter
• Fee Structure
• PAN Card
• Aadhaar Card
• Address Proof
• Academic Records
• Passport-size Photographs
• Income Proof of Co-applicant
• Bank Statements

-----------------------------------------

13. Co-applicant

A financially responsible co-applicant is generally mandatory.

Examples:

• Parent
• Guardian
• Spouse

-----------------------------------------

14. Credit Assessment

The following factors are considered:

• Reputation of Institution
• Course Type
• Employment Prospects
• Co-applicant Income
• Existing Financial Obligations

-----------------------------------------

15. Repayment

Repayment is made through monthly EMIs after the moratorium period.

-----------------------------------------

16. Prepayment

Customers may prepay part or the entire loan according to bank policy.

-----------------------------------------

17. Common Reasons for Rejection

• Unrecognized Institution
• Poor Co-applicant Financial Profile
• Incomplete Documents
• Failed KYC
• Fraud Indicators
• Admission Not Verified

-----------------------------------------

18. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is collateral required?

Depends on the loan amount and bank policy.

Q. Can I study abroad?

Yes, if the university is recognized and eligible.

Q. Can I repay before course completion?

Yes, voluntary repayment is usually permitted.

Q. Can my parent be the co-applicant?

Yes.

Q. Does the bank finance hostel fees?

Yes, subject to policy.
