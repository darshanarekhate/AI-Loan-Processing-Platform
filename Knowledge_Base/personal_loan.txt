=========================================
PERSONAL LOAN POLICY
=========================================

1. Introduction

A Personal Loan is an unsecured loan provided to customers for personal financial needs such as medical expenses, education, travel, marriage, home renovation, debt consolidation, or other approved purposes.

No collateral is generally required.

-----------------------------------------

2. Eligible Applicants

• Salaried Employees
• Self-employed Professionals
• Business Owners
• Pensioners (subject to bank policy)

-----------------------------------------

3. Minimum Age

21 Years

-----------------------------------------

4. Maximum Age at Loan Maturity

65 Years

-----------------------------------------

5. Minimum Monthly Income

₹20,000

Higher income generally increases loan eligibility.

-----------------------------------------

6. Loan Amount

Typical range:

Minimum:
₹50,000

Maximum:
₹25,00,000

The approved amount depends on:

• Monthly income
• Credit score
• Existing loan obligations
• Repayment capacity
• Employer profile

-----------------------------------------

7. Loan Tenure

Minimum:
12 Months

Maximum:
60 Months

-----------------------------------------

8. Interest Rate

Interest rates depend on:

• Credit score
• Employment type
• Income
• Loan amount
• Internal bank policy

-----------------------------------------

9. Processing Fee

Usually between

0.5% to 2% of the sanctioned loan amount.

-----------------------------------------

10. Required Documents

• PAN Card
• Aadhaar Card
• Passport-size Photograph
• Address Proof
• Salary Slips
• Last 6 Months Bank Statement
• Form 16 or Income Tax Return (where applicable)

-----------------------------------------

11. Credit Score Requirement

Excellent:
750+

Good:
700–749

Fair:
650–699

Below 650 may require manual review or may be declined.

-----------------------------------------

12. Repayment

Repayment is made through fixed monthly EMIs.

Each EMI consists of:

• Principal
• Interest

-----------------------------------------

13. Prepayment

Partial and full prepayment may be allowed.

Applicable charges depend on the loan agreement.

-----------------------------------------

14. Foreclosure

Customers may close the loan before maturity subject to foreclosure rules and applicable charges.

-----------------------------------------

15. Common Reasons for Rejection

• Low credit score
• High debt-to-income ratio
• Unstable employment
• Insufficient income
• Failed KYC verification
• Fraud indicators
• Incorrect or incomplete documents

-----------------------------------------

16. Benefits

• No collateral required
• Fast processing
• Flexible repayment
• Quick disbursement
• Online application available

-----------------------------------------

17. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is collateral required?

No.

Q. Can I apply online?

Yes.

Q. Can I prepay the loan?

Yes, subject to bank policy.

Q. Can self-employed customers apply?

Yes.

Q. Will my credit score affect approval?

Yes. A higher credit score generally improves approval chances and may result in better interest rates.
