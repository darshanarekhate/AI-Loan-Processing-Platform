AI Loan Approval Policy

Home Loan
- Minimum credit score: 700
- Maximum tenure: 30 years
- Income should support EMI.

Personal Loan
- Minimum credit score: 720
- Maximum tenure: 5 years

Car Loan
- Minimum credit score: 680
- Maximum tenure: 7 years

Education Loan
- Co-applicant required.
- Admission proof required.

General Rules

Applicants with VERIFIED KYC receive faster processing.

Applicants with HIGH fraud risk are rejected.

Applicants with MEDIUM fraud risk require manual review.

Loan approval also considers:

- Monthly income
- Existing obligations
- Fraud detection
- AI recommendation
