=========================================
HOME LOAN POLICY
=========================================

1. Introduction

A Home Loan helps customers purchase, construct, renovate, or extend residential property.

The bank provides flexible repayment options, competitive interest rates, and long repayment tenures.

-----------------------------------------

2. Eligible Applicants

• Salaried Employees
• Self-employed Professionals
• Business Owners
• Pensioners (subject to policy)

-----------------------------------------

3. Minimum Age

21 years

-----------------------------------------

4. Maximum Age at Loan Maturity

70 years

-----------------------------------------

5. Minimum Monthly Income

₹25,000

Higher income improves loan eligibility.

-----------------------------------------

6. Maximum Loan Amount

Based on:

• Monthly income
• Existing liabilities
• Credit score
• Property valuation
• Repayment capacity

-----------------------------------------

7. Loan Tenure

Minimum:
5 Years

Maximum:
30 Years

-----------------------------------------

8. Interest Type

• Fixed Interest Rate
• Floating Interest Rate

-----------------------------------------

9. Processing Fee

Typically between
0.25% and 1% of loan amount.

-----------------------------------------

10. Required Documents

• PAN Card
• Aadhaar Card
• Address Proof
• Income Proof
• Salary Slips
• Bank Statements
• Property Documents
• Passport-size Photographs

-----------------------------------------

11. Credit Score Requirement

Preferred:

750+

Applications below 650 may require additional review.

-----------------------------------------

12. Property Types

• Ready-to-move houses
• Apartments
• Independent villas
• Residential plots (where applicable)

-----------------------------------------

13. Repayment

Monthly EMI consists of:

• Principal
• Interest

-----------------------------------------

14. Prepayment

Customers may partially or fully prepay the loan.

Charges depend on loan agreement.

-----------------------------------------

15. Foreclosure

Customers may close the loan before maturity subject to applicable rules.

-----------------------------------------

16. Insurance

Property insurance may be recommended.

-----------------------------------------

17. Common Reasons for Rejection

• Low credit score
• Poor repayment history
• Fake documents
• Insufficient income
• Failed KYC
• High fraud risk

-----------------------------------------

18. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can I apply jointly?

Yes.

Q. Can NRIs apply?

Subject to bank policy.

Q. Can I transfer my home loan?

Yes, depending on bank guidelines.

Q. Is collateral required?

The financed property generally serves as collateral.

Q. Can I increase my loan amount later?

Subject to eligibility and reassessment.
