=========================================
EMI (EQUATED MONTHLY INSTALLMENT) GUIDE
=========================================

1. Introduction

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is the fixed amount a borrower pays every month to repay a loan. Each EMI consists of two components:

• Principal Amount
• Interest Amount

EMIs continue until the entire loan is repaid.

-----------------------------------------

2. EMI Formula

The standard EMI formula is:

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)^N
      --------------------
      ((1 + R)^N − 1)

Where:

P = Loan Amount

R = Monthly Interest Rate

N = Loan Tenure (in months)

-----------------------------------------

3. Factors Affecting EMI

EMI depends on:

• Loan Amount
• Interest Rate
• Loan Tenure

Increasing the loan amount or interest rate generally increases the EMI.

Increasing the tenure usually reduces the monthly EMI but increases the total interest paid.

-----------------------------------------

4. Example Calculation

Loan Amount:
₹10,00,000

Interest Rate:
8.5% per annum

Loan Tenure:
20 Years

The EMI is calculated using the bank's approved EMI formula.

The exact amount depends on the applicable interest rate and repayment schedule.

-----------------------------------------

5. EMI Payment Methods

Customers can pay EMIs through:

• Auto Debit
• ECS
• NACH
• Net Banking
• UPI (where supported)
• Standing Instructions

-----------------------------------------

6. EMI Due Date

EMIs are due on the scheduled repayment date mentioned in the loan agreement.

Late payments may attract penalties or additional interest.

-----------------------------------------

7. Missed EMI

Missing an EMI may result in:

• Late payment charges
• Penal interest
• Lower credit score
• Collection activities
• Higher repayment burden

Repeated missed EMIs may lead to loan default.

-----------------------------------------

8. Prepayment

Customers may partially prepay their loan before the scheduled tenure.

Benefits include:

• Lower outstanding principal
• Reduced total interest
• Faster loan closure

Prepayment charges, if applicable, depend on bank policy.

-----------------------------------------

9. Foreclosure

Loan foreclosure means repaying the outstanding loan balance before the original tenure ends.

Applicable charges depend on the loan agreement and bank policy.

-----------------------------------------

10. EMI Schedule

An EMI schedule typically includes:

• Installment Number
• Due Date
• Principal Component
• Interest Component
• Outstanding Balance

-----------------------------------------

11. Fixed vs Floating Interest Rate

Fixed Interest Rate:

• EMI generally remains constant throughout the tenure.

Floating Interest Rate:

• EMI or tenure may change when benchmark interest rates change.

-----------------------------------------

12. Improving EMI Affordability

Customers can reduce repayment burden by:

• Choosing a longer tenure
• Making a higher down payment
• Improving credit score
• Making partial prepayments
• Selecting suitable loan products

-----------------------------------------

13. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What does EMI stand for?

Equated Monthly Installment.

Q. Can EMI change during the loan tenure?

Yes, for floating-rate loans. Fixed-rate loans usually have constant EMIs.

Q. Can I pay more than my EMI?

Yes. Additional payments may reduce the outstanding principal, subject to bank policy.

Q. What happens if I miss an EMI?

Late charges, penal interest, and negative impact on your credit score may occur.

Q. Does a longer tenure reduce EMI?

Yes. However, a longer tenure generally increases the total interest paid over the life of the loan.
