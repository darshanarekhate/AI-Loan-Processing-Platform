=========================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
=========================================

1. General Questions

Q. What types of loans are supported?

The system provides information about:
• Home Loans
• Personal Loans
• Car Loans
• Education Loans

-----------------------------------------

Q. How do I apply for a loan?

Submit the required application form along with identity, address, and income documents. The application then undergoes verification and assessment.

-----------------------------------------

Q. How long does loan approval take?

Processing time depends on document verification, KYC completion, fraud assessment, and internal review. Complete and accurate applications are usually processed faster.

-----------------------------------------

2. Eligibility

Q. What is the minimum age to apply?

Most loan products require applicants to be at least 21 years old.

-----------------------------------------

Q. What is the maximum age?

The maximum age at loan maturity is generally between 65 and 70 years, depending on the loan product.

-----------------------------------------

Q. Does my salary affect eligibility?

Yes. Stable income and repayment capacity are important factors in loan assessment.

-----------------------------------------

Q. Does my credit score matter?

Yes. A higher credit score generally improves loan eligibility and may help secure better interest rates.

-----------------------------------------

3. Documents

Q. What documents are required?

Commonly required documents include:
• PAN Card
• Aadhaar Card
• Address Proof
• Income Proof
• Bank Statements
• Passport-size Photograph

Additional documents may be needed depending on the loan type.

-----------------------------------------

Q. Can I upload scanned copies?

Yes, provided they are clear, complete, and readable.

-----------------------------------------

Q. What happens if my document is unclear?

The OCR system may not extract information accurately, and the application could require manual verification.

-----------------------------------------

4. OCR

Q. What is OCR?

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) extracts text from uploaded documents and converts it into machine-readable data.

-----------------------------------------

Q. Why is OCR confidence important?

A higher confidence score indicates more reliable text extraction. Low confidence may require manual review.

-----------------------------------------

5. KYC

Q. What does VERIFIED mean?

All required identity details have matched successfully.

-----------------------------------------

Q. What does MANUAL REVIEW mean?

Additional verification by a bank officer is required before processing continues.

-----------------------------------------

Q. What causes KYC failure?

Common reasons include:
• PAN mismatch
• Aadhaar mismatch
• Name mismatch
• Incorrect Date of Birth
• Poor document quality

-----------------------------------------

6. Fraud Detection

Q. What is a fraud score?

The fraud score estimates the likelihood of fraudulent activity. Lower scores generally indicate lower risk.

-----------------------------------------

Q. Does a low fraud score guarantee approval?

No. Fraud assessment is only one part of the overall evaluation.

-----------------------------------------

Q. Why was my application reviewed manually?

Manual review may occur when verification results are incomplete or additional checks are required.

-----------------------------------------

7. AI Loan Decision

Q. Does AI approve my loan?

No. AI provides a recommendation to assist the bank. The final decision follows the bank's review process and lending policies.

-----------------------------------------

Q. Why was my application rejected?

Possible reasons include:
• Low credit score
• Insufficient income
• Failed KYC
• High fraud risk
• Incomplete documentation
• Policy non-compliance

-----------------------------------------

8. EMI

Q. What is an EMI?

An EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed monthly amount paid to repay a loan.

-----------------------------------------

Q. Can I repay my loan early?

Many loan products allow partial prepayment or foreclosure, subject to the terms and conditions of the loan agreement.

-----------------------------------------

9. Interest Rates

Q. Are interest rates fixed?

Some loans have fixed interest rates, while others use floating rates that may change over time.

-----------------------------------------

Q. How can I get a better interest rate?

A strong credit score, stable income, and good repayment history may help you qualify for more competitive rates.

-----------------------------------------

10. Support

Q. What should I do if my application is under manual review?

Wait for the bank to complete additional verification. You may be contacted if further documents or information are required.

-----------------------------------------

Q. Can I update my documents after submission?

Yes, if the bank requests revised or additional documents during the verification process.

-----------------------------------------

Q. How can I improve my chances of approval?

You can improve your application by:
• Maintaining a good credit score
• Providing accurate information
• Uploading clear documents
• Completing KYC successfully
• Ensuring stable income
• Reducing existing debt where possible
