=========================================
FRAUD DETECTION AND RISK ASSESSMENT GUIDE
=========================================

1. Introduction

Fraud detection is an important part of the loan approval process. The bank evaluates every loan application for suspicious activity before making a lending decision.

The objective is to protect customers, reduce financial risk, and comply with regulatory requirements.

-----------------------------------------

2. Purpose of Fraud Detection

Fraud analysis helps to:

• Identify suspicious applications
• Detect forged or altered documents
• Prevent identity theft
• Reduce financial losses
• Support regulatory compliance

-----------------------------------------

3. Fraud Assessment Process

The fraud detection process may include:

• Document verification
• OCR validation
• KYC verification
• Identity consistency checks
• Duplicate application detection
• Risk rule evaluation

The results contribute to the overall loan assessment.

-----------------------------------------

4. Fraud Status

Typical fraud outcomes include:

NO_FRAUD

No suspicious activity was detected.

POTENTIAL_FRAUD

Some indicators require additional investigation.

FRAUD_CONFIRMED

The application contains confirmed fraudulent indicators and is likely to be rejected.

-----------------------------------------

5. Risk Levels

LOW

Minimal fraud indicators.

MEDIUM

Some unusual patterns were detected. Manual review may be required.

HIGH

Strong fraud indicators are present. Additional verification is required before any lending decision.

-----------------------------------------

6. Fraud Score

The fraud score represents the likelihood that an application contains fraudulent activity.

General interpretation:

0 – 20
Very Low Risk

21 – 40
Low Risk

41 – 60
Medium Risk

61 – 80
High Risk

81 – 100
Very High Risk

A lower fraud score generally indicates lower fraud risk.

-----------------------------------------

7. Common Fraud Indicators

Examples include:

• Identity mismatch
• PAN mismatch
• Aadhaar mismatch
• Fake documents
• Altered documents
• Duplicate applications
• Suspicious income information
• Invalid identity details
• Multiple failed verification attempts

-----------------------------------------

8. Relationship with KYC

Fraud detection uses KYC verification results as one of its inputs.

Successful KYC reduces fraud risk but does not guarantee approval.

-----------------------------------------

9. Relationship with OCR

OCR helps extract document information.

If OCR detects inconsistent information or low-confidence extraction, additional fraud checks may be performed.

-----------------------------------------

10. Manual Review

Applications may be referred for manual review when:

• Medium fraud risk is detected
• Information is inconsistent
• Documents require additional verification
• Automated checks are inconclusive

-----------------------------------------

11. Customer Recommendations

Customers should:

• Submit genuine documents
• Ensure all information is accurate
• Avoid duplicate applications
• Upload clear images
• Complete KYC successfully

-----------------------------------------

12. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What does NO_FRAUD mean?

No suspicious activity was detected during the fraud assessment.

Q. Does a low fraud score guarantee loan approval?

No. Fraud analysis is only one part of the overall loan evaluation.

Q. Why was my application sent for manual review?

Manual review may occur when verification results are inconclusive or additional checks are required.

Q. Can OCR errors increase fraud risk?

Poor-quality documents or incorrect OCR extraction may lead to additional verification, but they do not automatically indicate fraud.

Q. Is fraud detection fully automated?

The system performs automated checks, but bank officers may conduct manual reviews when necessary.
