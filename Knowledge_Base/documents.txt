=========================================
LOAN DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS
=========================================

1. Introduction

Applicants must submit valid and complete documents for identity verification, eligibility assessment, income verification, and loan processing. Missing or incorrect documents may delay approval or result in rejection.

-----------------------------------------

2. Mandatory Identity Documents

At least one valid government-issued identity proof is required.

Accepted documents include:

• Aadhaar Card
• PAN Card
• Passport
• Voter ID
• Driving Licence

-----------------------------------------

3. Address Proof

Applicants must provide a valid address proof.

Accepted documents include:

• Aadhaar Card
• Passport
• Utility Bill
• Driving Licence
• Bank Passbook
• Rental Agreement (where accepted)

-----------------------------------------

4. PAN Card

PAN Card is generally mandatory for:

• Income verification
• Tax compliance
• High-value loans
• Credit assessment

-----------------------------------------

5. Aadhaar Card

Aadhaar is commonly used for:

• Identity verification
• Address verification
• KYC processing

-----------------------------------------

6. Income Proof

For Salaried Employees:

• Last 3 Salary Slips
• Form 16 (if applicable)
• Salary Certificate

For Self-employed Applicants:

• Income Tax Returns
• Profit & Loss Statement
• Balance Sheet
• Business Registration Documents

-----------------------------------------

7. Bank Statements

Applicants are generally required to provide:

• Last 6 months Bank Statements

These are used to evaluate:

• Salary credits
• Income consistency
• Existing EMIs
• Financial behaviour

-----------------------------------------

8. Photographs

Recent passport-size photographs may be required during application processing.

-----------------------------------------

9. Property Documents (Home Loan)

Examples include:

• Sale Agreement
• Title Deed
• Property Tax Receipt
• Approved Building Plan
• Encumbrance Certificate

-----------------------------------------

10. Vehicle Documents (Car Loan)

Examples include:

• Vehicle Quotation
• Dealer Invoice
• Insurance Details
• Registration Documents

-----------------------------------------

11. Education Loan Documents

Examples include:

• Admission Letter
• Fee Structure
• Academic Certificates
• Entrance Exam Scorecard
• Co-applicant Income Proof

-----------------------------------------

12. KYC Documents

KYC verification generally requires:

• PAN Card
• Aadhaar Card
• Photograph
• Address Proof

-----------------------------------------

13. OCR Verification

Uploaded documents may undergo Optical Character Recognition (OCR).

The system extracts information such as:

• Name
• PAN Number
• Aadhaar Number
• Date of Birth

The extracted data is compared against customer records.

-----------------------------------------

14. Common Reasons for Document Rejection

• Blurred image
• Expired document
• Incorrect document
• Cropped upload
• Mismatch in customer details
• Illegible text
• Fake or altered document

-----------------------------------------

15. Tips for Uploading Documents

• Upload clear colour images
• Avoid glare and shadows
• Capture the complete document
• Ensure all text is readable
• Upload original documents only

-----------------------------------------

16. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is PAN mandatory?

Yes, for most loan products.

Q. Is Aadhaar mandatory?

It is generally required for KYC verification.

Q. Can I upload scanned copies?

Yes, provided they are clear and readable.

Q. Can I submit documents later?

Processing may begin only after the required documents are received.

Q. What happens if OCR cannot read my document?

The application may be sent for manual verification or the customer may be asked to upload a clearer copy.
