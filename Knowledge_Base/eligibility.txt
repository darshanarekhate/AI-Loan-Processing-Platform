=========================================
LOAN ELIGIBILITY GUIDELINES
=========================================

1. Introduction

Loan eligibility determines whether a customer qualifies for a loan based on financial, personal, and regulatory criteria. Every loan application undergoes an eligibility assessment before approval.

-----------------------------------------

2. Basic Eligibility

Applicants should:

• Be an Indian citizen (unless specific NRI products apply)
• Meet the minimum age requirement
• Have a stable source of income
• Complete KYC verification
• Meet the bank's credit policy

-----------------------------------------

3. Minimum Age

Most loan products require:

Minimum Age:
21 Years

Some education loans may allow applicants below 18 years with a parent or guardian as co-applicant.

-----------------------------------------

4. Maximum Age

Maximum age at loan maturity generally ranges between:

65 to 70 years

depending on the loan product.

-----------------------------------------

5. Employment Categories

Eligible applicants may include:

• Salaried Employees
• Government Employees
• Private Sector Employees
• Self-employed Professionals
• Business Owners
• Pensioners (for selected products)

-----------------------------------------

6. Income Requirement

Minimum income depends on the loan product.

Examples:

Home Loan:
₹25,000 per month

Personal Loan:
₹20,000 per month

Higher income generally increases loan eligibility.

-----------------------------------------

7. Credit Score

Credit score plays an important role in loan approval.

Typical guidelines:

750 and above:
Excellent

700–749:
Good

650–699:
Fair

Below 650:
May require manual review or may be declined depending on bank policy.

-----------------------------------------

8. Existing Loan Obligations

The bank evaluates:

• Existing EMIs
• Outstanding loans
• Credit card dues
• Debt-to-income ratio

Higher liabilities may reduce loan eligibility.

-----------------------------------------

9. Employment Stability

Applicants with stable employment and consistent income generally have stronger eligibility.

The bank may consider:

• Employer profile
• Years of service
• Business stability
• Income consistency

-----------------------------------------

10. Required Documents

Eligibility assessment normally requires:

• PAN Card
• Aadhaar Card
• Address Proof
• Income Proof
• Bank Statements
• Passport-size Photograph

Additional documents may be requested depending on the loan type.

-----------------------------------------

11. KYC Verification

Successful KYC verification is mandatory before loan approval.

Applications with failed or incomplete KYC may proceed to manual review or be rejected.

-----------------------------------------

12. OCR Validation

Uploaded documents are processed using OCR.

The extracted details are compared with customer records to identify mismatches.

-----------------------------------------

13. Fraud Assessment

Fraud detection checks for suspicious activities such as:

• Forged documents
• Identity mismatch
• Duplicate applications
• High-risk patterns

High fraud risk may result in rejection or manual review.

-----------------------------------------

14. AI Loan Assessment

The AI-assisted decision engine evaluates:

• Credit score
• Income
• KYC results
• OCR results
• Fraud analysis
• Internal policy rules

The AI recommendation supports the final lending decision.

-----------------------------------------

15. Reasons for Ineligibility

Applicants may be considered ineligible due to:

• Low credit score
• Insufficient income
• High existing debt
• Failed KYC
• Fraud indicators
• Incomplete documentation
• Invalid identity documents

-----------------------------------------

16. Improving Eligibility

Applicants may improve eligibility by:

• Maintaining a good credit score
• Paying EMIs on time
• Reducing outstanding debt
• Providing accurate documents
• Completing KYC successfully
• Maintaining stable employment

-----------------------------------------

17. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Does a higher salary improve eligibility?

Yes. Higher and stable income generally improves repayment capacity.

Q. Can I apply with a low credit score?

Yes, but the application may require manual review and additional verification.

Q. Can self-employed applicants apply?

Yes, subject to income and document verification.

Q. Does successful KYC guarantee loan approval?

No. KYC is only one part of the overall eligibility assessment.

Q. Does AI make the final approval decision?

No. AI provides a recommendation that supports the bank's lending process.
