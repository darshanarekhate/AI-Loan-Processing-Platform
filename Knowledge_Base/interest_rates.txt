=========================================
LOAN INTEREST RATES GUIDE
=========================================

1. Introduction

Interest is the amount charged by the bank for borrowing money. The interest rate determines how much the borrower pays in addition to the principal amount.

Interest rates vary depending on the loan product, customer profile, credit score, market conditions, and internal bank policies.

-----------------------------------------

2. Types of Interest Rates

The bank may offer:

• Fixed Interest Rate
• Floating Interest Rate

-----------------------------------------

3. Fixed Interest Rate

A fixed interest rate remains unchanged throughout the agreed loan period.

Advantages:

• Predictable EMI
• Stable repayment amount
• Easier financial planning

Disadvantages:

• Customers may not benefit if market interest rates decrease.

-----------------------------------------

4. Floating Interest Rate

A floating interest rate changes according to benchmark lending rates and market conditions.

Advantages:

• Customers may benefit when market rates fall.

Disadvantages:

• EMI or loan tenure may increase if market rates rise.

-----------------------------------------

5. Home Loan Interest Rate

Typical Range:

8.00% – 10.50% per annum

Actual rates depend on:

• Credit score
• Income
• Loan amount
• Loan tenure
• Property profile
• Bank policy

-----------------------------------------

6. Personal Loan Interest Rate

Typical Range:

10.50% – 18.00% per annum

Personal loans usually carry higher interest rates because they are unsecured.

-----------------------------------------

7. Car Loan Interest Rate

Typical Range:

8.50% – 12.50% per annum

Interest depends on:

• Vehicle type
• New or used vehicle
• Credit profile
• Loan tenure

-----------------------------------------

8. Education Loan Interest Rate

Typical Range:

8.00% – 13.00% per annum

Rates may vary based on:

• Institution
• Course
• Loan amount
• Government subsidy schemes
• Bank policy

-----------------------------------------

9. Factors Affecting Interest Rate

The bank evaluates:

• Credit score
• Monthly income
• Employment stability
• Existing loan obligations
• Loan amount
• Loan tenure
• Relationship with the bank
• Internal risk assessment

-----------------------------------------

10. Credit Score Impact

Excellent (750+)

May qualify for lower interest rates.

Good (700–749)

Generally eligible for competitive rates.

Fair (650–699)

May receive standard rates.

Below 650

Higher interest rate or manual review may apply.

-----------------------------------------

11. Interest Calculation

Interest is calculated according to the loan agreement and repayment schedule.

The total interest payable depends on:

• Outstanding principal
• Interest rate
• Loan tenure
• Repayment behavior

-----------------------------------------

12. Penal Interest

If a customer misses scheduled EMI payments, the bank may apply penal interest according to the loan agreement.

-----------------------------------------

13. Reducing Interest Cost

Customers may reduce overall interest by:

• Maintaining a strong credit score
• Choosing a shorter tenure
• Making partial prepayments
• Paying EMIs on time
• Avoiding loan defaults

-----------------------------------------

14. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Which loan generally has the lowest interest rate?

Home loans generally offer lower interest rates because they are secured by property.

Q. Can my interest rate change?

Yes, if the loan uses a floating interest rate.

Q. Does a better credit score reduce interest rates?

Generally, yes. Customers with higher credit scores often receive more competitive interest rates.

Q. Can I switch from floating to fixed interest?

This depends on the bank's policy and the terms of your loan agreement.

Q. Does paying EMIs on time help?

Yes. Timely repayment supports a healthy credit profile and may improve future borrowing opportunities.
